How it works

  1. Describe.

    What the AI does, where it is used and who it affects. Only the questions that matter for your case appear.

    Thirteen AI and data regulations are tested against your answers. Each one applies, may apply, or does not.

  3. Decide.

    Yes, yes with conditions, or no. Every condition names the law and the section it comes from.

Every answer cites the law it comes from. The article, the section, and a link to the original text. No black box, and no guessing.
